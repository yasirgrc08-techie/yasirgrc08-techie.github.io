Privacy & scope
Your CV, pasted job description, and PDF rendering stay on this device. Local drafts are not encrypted cloud backups and can be lost if browser storage is cleared.
When configured, checkout sends payment details to Razorpay. The verification service receives order/payment proofs, not your CV. No AI model receives your resume.
This is an independent CV editor with original templates, not Overleaf or an employer-endorsed product. It does not provide cloud accounts, simultaneous collaboration, or online LaTeX compilation.
