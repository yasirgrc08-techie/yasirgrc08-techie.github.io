# A Complete 12-Week SWE Study Plan / Build and Validate

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Should all subjects receive equal time?

Connect topics through one small service instead of completing unrelated courses. Algorithms teach efficient computation; OS and networks explain execution; databases preserve state; design coordinates the pieces. The project makes gaps visible while mocks test whether the learning transfers.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Set up the scenario**: Build a service that creates tasks, stores their status, and runs a simple background operation.
2. **Experiment 1**: Define task ID, owner, status, and timestamps. Specify creation, status lookup, and cancellation behavior before coding.
3. **Experiment 2**: Add a worker that claims a queued task and records completion.
4. **Experiment 3**: Stop the worker during a task, restart it, and explain the recovery behavior.
5. **Expected evidence**: One small service demonstrates coding, schema design, concurrency, networking, testing, and operational reasoning.
6. **Check the result**: Can a new reader reproduce the service without private configuration?
