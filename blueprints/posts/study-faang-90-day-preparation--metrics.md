# FAANG Preparation Without Question Chasing / Readiness Signals

## The Idea

How many problems should I solve?

Build transferable coding, design, and communication skills, then tailor the final revision to the actual team and level. Use official hiring guidance for scope, mocks for diagnosis, and a limited resource set for consistent practice. Avoid treating alleged question frequency as a hiring guarantee.
