# FAANG Preparation Without Question Chasing / Practice Decisions

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many problems should I solve?

Build transferable coding, design, and communication skills, then tailor the final revision to the actual team and level. Use official hiring guidance for scope, mocks for diagnosis, and a limited resource set for consistent practice. Avoid treating alleged question frequency as a hiring guarantee.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Common skills, different emphasis**: Coding fundamentals are reusable, but design depth, specialization, and behavioral expectations vary with level and team.
2. **Watch for**: Assuming Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, and Netflix run identical loops.
3. **Evidence over memorization**: A useful solution includes the problem contract, invariant, implementation, complexity, and tests. Change a constraint and see whether your reasoning still holds.
4. **Watch for**: Memorizing anecdotes instead of understanding invariants and trade-offs.
5. **Separate official scope from anecdotes**: Employer preparation pages and recruiter instructions describe the process more reliably than viral interview lists.
6. **Watch for**: Treating a preparation plan as an offer or placement guarantee.
