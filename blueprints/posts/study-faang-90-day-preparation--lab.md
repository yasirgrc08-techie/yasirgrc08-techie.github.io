# FAANG Preparation Without Question Chasing / Build and Validate

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many problems should I solve?

Build transferable coding, design, and communication skills, then tailor the final revision to the actual team and level. Use official hiring guidance for scope, mocks for diagnosis, and a limited resource set for consistent practice. Avoid treating alleged question frequency as a hiring guarantee.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Set up the scenario**: You are applying to an infrastructure role and a consumer-product backend role.
2. **Experiment 1**: Extract explicit requirements from both job descriptions: coding language, data structures, distributed services, databases, performance, or domain knowledge.
3. **Experiment 2**: Allocate most time to shared coding and engineering fundamentals.
4. **Experiment 3**: Choose one unseen coding mock and one tailored design per track.
5. **Expected evidence**: A shared core plus two narrow tracks uses time more efficiently than maintaining two unrelated question lists.
6. **Check the result**: Does every specialized topic connect to a stated role requirement?
