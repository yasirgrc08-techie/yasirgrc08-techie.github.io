# Build a Sustainable Engineering Study System / Pitfalls and Checks

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many hours should I study each day?

A useful study system turns limited time into durable understanding. Choose a small number of goals, practice retrieval and application, revisit mistakes, and adjust from evidence. Consistency should not mean ignoring rest or treating a missed day as failure of the whole plan.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Trap 1**: Planning from an ideal week with no interruptions or recovery.
2. **Counter-check 1**: Study blocks have observable learning outcomes.
3. **Trap 2**: Collecting resources instead of using a small set consistently.
4. **Counter-check 2**: Review includes delayed recall and unfamiliar variations.
5. **Trap 3**: Treating a missed session as a reason to abandon the entire plan.
6. **Counter-check 3**: The schedule can adapt to real obligations.
