# Move from Services to a Product Role / Build and Validate

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Do product companies reject services experience automatically?

A credible transition translates existing work into engineering evidence, closes concrete skill gaps, and shows ownership of a small end-to-end system. You do not need to invent a distributed platform or erase support experience; you need to explain decisions, impact, testing, and learning clearly.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Set up the scenario**: Translate current responsibilities into defensible engineering stories.
2. **Experiment 1**: State the recurring failure and the manual workflow it required. Identify your actual responsibility: diagnosis, script design, tests, rollout, or documentation.
3. **Experiment 2**: Describe the change and the evidence you possess. If you measured operator time on a few representative tasks, disclose that sample and method.
4. **Experiment 3**: Explain a trade-off, such as safety checks versus speed, and a failure case the script handles.
5. **Expected evidence**: A defensible bullet and interview story communicate ownership and judgment better than an inflated scale claim.
6. **Check the result**: Can you answer how the result was measured?
