# Run Better Mocks and Measure Readiness / Readiness Signals

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

What score means I am ready?

A mock is a diagnostic experiment. It should reveal how you clarify, reason, implement, test, communicate, and recover from feedback. Score these dimensions separately, then change one behavior and check it on a new task. A single pass or failure is not a reliable verdict.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Explain**: Recent mocks use unfamiliar tasks and recorded hints.
2. **Interpret / Explain**: Are hints and independent discoveries recorded separately?
3. **Implement**: Weak dimensions have specific, tested interventions.
4. **Interpret / Implement**: Does the candidate explain why each test matters?
5. **Verify**: Explanations remain coherent under reasonable follow-up changes.
6. **Interpret / Verify**: Does the corrected behavior persist across more than one session?
