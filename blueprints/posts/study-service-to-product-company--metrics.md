# Move from Services to a Product Role / Readiness Signals

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Do product companies reject services experience automatically?

A credible transition translates existing work into engineering evidence, closes concrete skill gaps, and shows ownership of a small end-to-end system. You do not need to invent a distributed platform or erase support experience; you need to explain decisions, impact, testing, and learning clearly.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Explain**: The target role family and top skill gaps are clear.
2. **Interpret / Explain**: Can you answer how the result was measured?
3. **Implement**: Resume claims can be explained and defended.
4. **Interpret / Implement**: Can you separate your change from the team's broader delivery?
5. **Verify**: A personal project demonstrates tests and failure handling.
6. **Interpret / Verify**: Would sharing the example respect employer and customer confidentiality?
