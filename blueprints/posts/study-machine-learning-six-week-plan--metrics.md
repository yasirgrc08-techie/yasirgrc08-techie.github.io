## The Idea

When should I avoid ML?

Learn ML as a decision-making pipeline rather than a catalogue of model names. Define the prediction time, label, objective, data split, baseline, and error costs before optimizing a model. Reliable evaluation and data quality usually matter more than an impressive architecture diagram.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Explain**: Prediction time, labels, and available features are explicit.
2. **Interpret / Explain**: Could every feature exist at the real prediction moment?
3. **Implement**: A simple baseline is evaluated fairly.
4. **Interpret / Implement**: Are evaluation labels mature and consistently defined?
5. **Verify**: Metrics and thresholds match error costs and constraints.
6. **Interpret / Verify**: Does the metric support the action the product will take?
