# Plan Your DSA Preparation in Eight Weeks / Readiness Signals

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Should I finish one topic completely before moving on?

Study data structures through the operations they support and algorithms through the invariants that make them correct. Use topic-focused practice to learn, then mixed unseen problems to test recognition. A complete preparation loop includes clarification, implementation, testing, complexity, and revision.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Explain**: Mixed unseen problems can be approached without category hints.
2. **Interpret / Explain**: Can you state the monotonic property in the positive-input version?
3. **Implement**: Each solution includes a clear invariant and complexity.
4. **Interpret / Implement**: Does your counterexample actually break the reused window rule?
5. **Verify**: Boundary cases are tested before completion is declared.
6. **Interpret / Verify**: Are empty results and impossible targets handled according to the problem?
