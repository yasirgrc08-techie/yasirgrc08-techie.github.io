# Incidents Need an Evidence Trail / Failure and Recovery

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Changing five things at once can restore service and erase the explanation.

During an incident, prioritize user impact and controlled mitigation while preserving enough evidence to distinguish causes and verify recovery.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Capture the first signal**: Record the alert, affected journey, and earliest known failure time.
2. **Separate observations from guesses**: Label hypotheses explicitly instead of treating the first explanation as fact.
3. **Choose a narrow mitigation**: Apply one authorized action with a clear expected effect.
4. **Watch the predicted signal**: Check whether the action changed the measurement the hypothesis predicted.
5. **Confirm user recovery**: Verify useful outcomes rather than only a green component dashboard.
6. **Preserve the learning**: Turn the controlling failure into a test, runbook improvement, or owned engineering task.
