# FAANG Preparation Without Question Chasing / Pitfalls and Checks

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many problems should I solve?

Build transferable coding, design, and communication skills, then tailor the final revision to the actual team and level. Use official hiring guidance for scope, mocks for diagnosis, and a limited resource set for consistent practice. Avoid treating alleged question frequency as a hiring guarantee.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Trap 1**: Assuming Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, and Netflix run identical loops.
2. **Counter-check 1**: Mixed coding mocks are independently correct and tested.
3. **Trap 2**: Memorizing anecdotes instead of understanding invariants and trade-offs.
4. **Counter-check 2**: Design assumptions and failure boundaries are explicit.
5. **Trap 3**: Treating a preparation plan as an offer or placement guarantee.
6. **Counter-check 3**: Behavioral stories reflect real personal contributions.
