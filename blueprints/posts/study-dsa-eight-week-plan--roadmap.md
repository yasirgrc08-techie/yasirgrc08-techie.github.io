# Plan Your DSA Preparation in Eight Weeks / Learning Roadmap

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Should I finish one topic completely before moving on?

Study data structures through the operations they support and algorithms through the invariants that make them correct. Use topic-focused practice to learn, then mixed unseen problems to test recognition. A complete preparation loop includes clarification, implementation, testing, complexity, and revision.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Build the mental model**: Input size, ordering, value range, mutation rules, and output requirements change the solution.
2. **Phase 1 / Weeks 1-2**: Cover arrays, maps, sets, pointers, windows, stacks, and binary search. Trace indexes and counts explicitly.
3. **Phase 2 / Weeks 3-4**: Study recursion, iterative traversal, BFS, DFS, priority queues, connected components, and topological order.
4. **Phase 3 / Weeks 5-6**: Practice backtracking, greedy arguments, intervals, and dynamic programming.
5. **Phase 4 / Weeks 7-8**: Use unseen mixed sets and realistic time limits. Preserve time for tests and complexity.
6. **Prove readiness**: Mixed unseen problems can be approached without category hints.
