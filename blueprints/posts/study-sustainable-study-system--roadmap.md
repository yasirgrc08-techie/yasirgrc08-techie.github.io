# Build a Sustainable Engineering Study System / Learning Roadmap

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many hours should I study each day?

A useful study system turns limited time into durable understanding. Choose a small number of goals, practice retrieval and application, revisit mistakes, and adjust from evidence. Consistency should not mean ignoring rest or treating a missed day as failure of the whole plan.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Build the mental model**: Finishing videos or accumulating notes may support learning, but the useful outcome is what you can explain, implement, or decide independently.
2. **Phase 1 / Start of week**: Review the target role and recent mistakes. Choose one primary technical topic, one maintenance topic, and one small application task.
3. **Phase 2 / During sessions**: Begin by recalling the prior concept without notes. Work on a problem or lab, then compare the result with tests or a reliable reference.
4. **Phase 3 / Midweek**: Re-solve an earlier failure and try a related unfamiliar variant. Mix topics once the basics are understood so recognition is tested.
5. **Phase 4 / End of week**: Inspect completed artifacts and recurring errors. Keep what worked, change one unhelpful habit, and reduce unnecessary resource switching.
6. **Prove readiness**: Study blocks have observable learning outcomes.
