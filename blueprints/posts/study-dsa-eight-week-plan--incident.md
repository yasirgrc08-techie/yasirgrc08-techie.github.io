# Plan Your DSA Preparation in Eight Weeks / Pitfalls and Checks

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Should I finish one topic completely before moving on?

Study data structures through the operations they support and algorithms through the invariants that make them correct. Use topic-focused practice to learn, then mixed unseen problems to test recognition. A complete preparation loop includes clarification, implementation, testing, complexity, and revision.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Trap 1**: Selecting a pattern from the title while ignoring constraints.
2. **Counter-check 1**: Mixed unseen problems can be approached without category hints.
3. **Trap 2**: Counting copied solutions as independent practice.
4. **Counter-check 2**: Each solution includes a clear invariant and complexity.
5. **Trap 3**: Skipping correctness reasoning and tests to maximize volume.
6. **Counter-check 3**: Boundary cases are tested before completion is declared.
