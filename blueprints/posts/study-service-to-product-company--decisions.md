# Move from Services to a Product Role / Practice Decisions

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Do product companies reject services experience automatically?

A credible transition translates existing work into engineering evidence, closes concrete skill gaps, and shows ownership of a small end-to-end system. You do not need to invent a distributed platform or erase support experience; you need to explain decisions, impact, testing, and learning clearly.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Translate, do not exaggerate**: Maintenance work can show debugging, reliability, migration, customer communication, and risk management. Describe what you actually changed and how you verified it.
2. **Watch for**: Disparaging current work instead of extracting its transferable value.
3. **Pick a role family**: Backend, frontend, mobile, infrastructure, and ML roles have different depth requirements.
4. **Watch for**: Fabricating scale, ownership, or measured impact on the resume.
5. **Make artifacts inspectable**: A recruiter may skim a resume, while an interviewer probes a project. Maintain a concise README, architecture sketch, tests, issue history, and a small demo.
6. **Watch for**: Publishing employer code or using an unsustainable preparation schedule.
