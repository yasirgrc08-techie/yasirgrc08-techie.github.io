# Run Better Mocks and Measure Readiness / Practice Decisions

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

What score means I am ready?

A mock is a diagnostic experiment. It should reveal how you clarify, reason, implement, test, communicate, and recover from feedback. Score these dimensions separately, then change one behavior and check it on a new task. A single pass or failure is not a reliable verdict.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Keep the task unfamiliar**: Choose an unseen problem or change the constraints of a known one. Hide the pattern category.
2. **Watch for**: Repeating familiar problems and counting them as unseen mocks.
3. **Separate dimensions**: A correct answer may still have weak testing or poor explanation.
4. **Watch for**: Judging only speed while ignoring correctness, tests, and clarity.
5. **Measure a trend**: Use several sessions under similar conditions. Record hints and interruptions so comparisons are fair.
6. **Watch for**: Running frequent mocks without allocating time to fix the failures.
