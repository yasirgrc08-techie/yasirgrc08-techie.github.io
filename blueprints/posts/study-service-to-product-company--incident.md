# Move from Services to a Product Role / Pitfalls and Checks

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Do product companies reject services experience automatically?

A credible transition translates existing work into engineering evidence, closes concrete skill gaps, and shows ownership of a small end-to-end system. You do not need to invent a distributed platform or erase support experience; you need to explain decisions, impact, testing, and learning clearly.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Trap 1**: Disparaging current work instead of extracting its transferable value.
2. **Counter-check 1**: The target role family and top skill gaps are clear.
3. **Trap 2**: Fabricating scale, ownership, or measured impact on the resume.
4. **Counter-check 2**: Resume claims can be explained and defended.
5. **Trap 3**: Publishing employer code or using an unsustainable preparation schedule.
6. **Counter-check 3**: A personal project demonstrates tests and failure handling.
