# Build a Sustainable Engineering Study System / Practice Decisions

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many hours should I study each day?

A useful study system turns limited time into durable understanding. Choose a small number of goals, practice retrieval and application, revisit mistakes, and adjust from evidence. Consistency should not mean ignoring rest or treating a missed day as failure of the whole plan.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Choose an outcome, not an activity count**: Finishing videos or accumulating notes may support learning, but the useful outcome is what you can explain, implement, or decide independently.
2. **Watch for**: Planning from an ideal week with no interruptions or recovery.
3. **Recall and transfer expose gaps**: Attempt an explanation or problem without notes before reviewing. Revisit weak material after a delay and apply it in a changed context.
4. **Watch for**: Collecting resources instead of using a small set consistently.
5. **A realistic system survives interruptions**: Set a minimum viable session and reserve slack. When work expands, reduce optional breadth rather than compressing every missed task into a punishing weekend.
6. **Watch for**: Treating a missed session as a reason to abandon the entire plan.
