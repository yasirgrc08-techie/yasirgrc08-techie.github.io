# Explain Technical Work Clearly / Practice Decisions

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How short should a technical summary be?

Good technical communication helps someone make a decision or reproduce a result. Start with the audience and question, separate facts from hypotheses, and choose the smallest structure that preserves the important reasoning. A concise explanation can still include assumptions, evidence, and limitations.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Lead with the decision or outcome**: A reviewer needs to know what changed and why before reading implementation detail. A stakeholder needs impact and next action. An interviewer needs your reasoning.
2. **Watch for**: Using jargon to hide an unclear mental model.
3. **Separate evidence from inference**: A log line is an observation; a root-cause explanation is an inference that should be tested. Label assumptions and unresolved questions.
4. **Watch for**: Mixing proposed, implemented, and verified work in one claim.
5. **Use structure to expose causality**: A before/after statement, sequence diagram, table, or short timeline can make ownership and order visible.
6. **Watch for**: Describing chronology without explaining cause and effect.
