# Move from Services to a Product Role / Learning Roadmap

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Do product companies reject services experience automatically?

A credible transition translates existing work into engineering evidence, closes concrete skill gaps, and shows ownership of a small end-to-end system. You do not need to invent a distributed platform or erase support experience; you need to explain decisions, impact, testing, and learning clearly.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Build the mental model**: Maintenance work can show debugging, reliability, migration, customer communication, and risk management. Describe what you actually changed and how you verified it.
2. **Phase 1 / Weeks 1-3**: Map your current work to debugging, design, testing, and ownership skills.
3. **Phase 2 / Weeks 4-7**: Practice coding patterns and the relevant stack. For backend roles, add SQL transactions, API validation, HTTP, and failure handling.
4. **Phase 3 / Weeks 8-11**: Add one non-happy-path capability: retries, pagination, authorization, migrations, or observability. Measure a real property with a reproducible workload.
5. **Phase 4 / Weeks 12-16**: Use official applications and relevant professional contacts. Track role fit, application stage, and interview feedback.
6. **Prove readiness**: The target role family and top skill gaps are clear.
