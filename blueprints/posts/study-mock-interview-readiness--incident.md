# Run Better Mocks and Measure Readiness / Pitfalls and Checks

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

What score means I am ready?

A mock is a diagnostic experiment. It should reveal how you clarify, reason, implement, test, communicate, and recover from feedback. Score these dimensions separately, then change one behavior and check it on a new task. A single pass or failure is not a reliable verdict.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Trap 1**: Repeating familiar problems and counting them as unseen mocks.
2. **Counter-check 1**: Recent mocks use unfamiliar tasks and recorded hints.
3. **Trap 2**: Judging only speed while ignoring correctness, tests, and clarity.
4. **Counter-check 2**: Weak dimensions have specific, tested interventions.
5. **Trap 3**: Running frequent mocks without allocating time to fix the failures.
6. **Counter-check 3**: Explanations remain coherent under reasonable follow-up changes.
