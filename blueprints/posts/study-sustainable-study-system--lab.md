# Build a Sustainable Engineering Study System / Build and Validate

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many hours should I study each day?

A useful study system turns limited time into durable understanding. Choose a small number of goals, practice retrieval and application, revisit mistakes, and adjust from evidence. Consistency should not mean ignoring rest or treating a missed day as failure of the whole plan.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Set up the scenario**: You planned ten hours but an unexpected work deadline leaves four. The original list contains algorithms, networking, a project feature, three videos, and a mock.
2. **Experiment 1**: Identify the week's most important demonstrated weakness, such as incorrect graph visited-state handling. Keep one focused practice block and one delayed re-solve.
3. **Experiment 2**: Choose one small project or explanation artifact that reinforces the same concept, and shorten the mock into a targeted unseen task if needed.
4. **Experiment 3**: At the end, record what was learned and what remains.
5. **Expected evidence**: The reduced week still strengthens one important skill and maintains continuity.
6. **Check the result**: Does the retained work address a specific observed weakness?
