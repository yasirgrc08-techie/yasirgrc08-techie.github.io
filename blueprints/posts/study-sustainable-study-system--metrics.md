# Build a Sustainable Engineering Study System / Readiness Signals

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

How many hours should I study each day?

A useful study system turns limited time into durable understanding. Choose a small number of goals, practice retrieval and application, revisit mistakes, and adjust from evidence. Consistency should not mean ignoring rest or treating a missed day as failure of the whole plan.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **Explain**: Study blocks have observable learning outcomes.
2. **Interpret / Explain**: Does the retained work address a specific observed weakness?
3. **Implement**: Review includes delayed recall and unfamiliar variations.
4. **Interpret / Implement**: Is there an independent check rather than only passive consumption?
5. **Verify**: The schedule can adapt to real obligations.
6. **Interpret / Verify**: Has the plan left reasonable recovery time and avoided unbounded backlog?
