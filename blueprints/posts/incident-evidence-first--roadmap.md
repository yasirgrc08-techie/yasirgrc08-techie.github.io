# Incidents Need an Evidence Trail / Bottleneck Roadmap

By Yasir Sharfi

## The Idea

Changing five things at once can restore service and erase the explanation.

During an incident, prioritize user impact and controlled mitigation while preserving enough evidence to distinguish causes and verify recovery.

## Visual Blueprint

1. **State the impact**: Identify affected users, operations, start time, and known boundaries.
2. **Assign clear roles**: Separate coordination, investigation, communication, and risky changes when team size permits.
3. **Build a timeline**: Record observations and actions with timestamps and source evidence.
4. **Test one useful hypothesis**: Choose a discriminating observation before broad changes.
5. **Mitigate reversibly**: Prefer bounded actions with known risks and explicit rollback conditions.
6. **Verify recovery and learn**: Check user outcomes, document uncertainty, and assign actionable follow-up tests.
